Project map · Four poles

When a screen drifts toward dashboard, music site, meme dump, or merch wall, the system corrects it back to the centre.

Music

Tracks govern atmosphere, families, queue continuity, station state. Music is not a promo attachment under a t-shirt page — it is first-class identity input.

Product

Myth

Storefront

Editorial, not dashboard. Player second. Meme contained. Premium first; commerce second; archive bounded; meme contained.

Non-negotiables

What must be preserved.

Five rules that hold across every page, every release, every direction.

Architectural

Home is the canonical shell. Covers are the variation engine. RADIO is a first-class station page family, not a replacement for home.

Editorial

Tone stays ceremonial, controlled, worldbuilding-led, sparse. Music is identity, not promo. Commerce never louder than atmosphere.

Composition

Every page has a strong primary focal zone. Reading order is framed, not floated. Figure-to-atmosphere separation must be clear.

Symbolic

Sigils are canonical, not decorative. Sacred geometry, medallions, rings, waveform traces are allowed as system language.

World model

Music, product, myth, storefront must stay related. No page collapses into one pole only.

The bottom line: this is a store-shaped cultural system, not a storefront with better art direction. The shell is shared, but module rank, light behaviour, and page atmosphere stop collapsing into one generic visual tone. — SORA · Founder & Signal Keeper
