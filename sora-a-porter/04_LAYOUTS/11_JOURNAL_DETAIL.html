Makoto Takemiya's tweetstorm

Breakdown of the 4 articles Makoto Takemiya posted during the week of May 10, 2026.

— SORA · Founder & Signal Keeper

02:00 Makoto Takemiya's tweetstorm · 06:16 06:16

From silence, the frequencies rise. We broadcast to those who collect meaning, not just music.

During the week of May 10, 2026, Makoto Takemiya — founder of Soramitsu — published four consecutive articles framing where SORA Nexus comes from and where it is going. This dispatch breaks them down without paraphrasing them: it places them next to each other.

The first article reads SORA's lineage backwards: from Iroha 3 down to Fisher's 1939 framing of a monetary system answerable to its users, and forward into the operating thesis behind SORA Nexus. It is not a roadmap — it is a charter.

The second article addresses Yamaguchi's economic critique and explains why SORA chose not to optimise for speculation. The tone is editorial, not promotional. It is the tone the Founder reserves for moments when something has to be said clearly and once.

The third article works as a diary entry: it documents what Nexus does today, in the present tense, as a node already broadcasting. The fourth closes the cycle — short, almost a postscript — and signs the week.

This is not background. This is transmission.

— SORA · Founder & Signal Keeper
